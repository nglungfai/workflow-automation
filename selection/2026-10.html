02

When Agent Evaluation Becomes Unreliable: An Empirical Study of Oracle Failures and Configuration-Dependent Measurement in an LLM Agent Benchmark

Junjie Chen (Nanjing Univ. of Science and Technology) · Oct 2026

Verdict. Included for a reusable, cheap check on any harness that grades agent runs. The trade-off: the primary evidence comes from one benchmark served by one provider, the method did not port to a second framework, and the language-configuration findings are explicitly weaker and one pairwise contrast is underpowered.

Scored by Claude (single pass, full text read). Second review: Independent blind pass, Opus, from the snapshot only (draft scores not shown); prompt blind-v1, 1 disagreement. Reviewed by the author.

  • Evidence basisStrong

    Reports observed data with denominators: 4,320 archived episodes (30 tasks, 4 agents, 6 simulators, 3 repetitions), 4,194 archived passes against 2,883 after replay, per-agent and per-task changes with task-clustered intervals, and a 3,240-episode ablation. The secondary language results are weaker and the paper says so.

    “recorded labels report 4,194 database passes over 4,320 episodes, replay gives 2,883”
  • Method transparencyPartial

    Steps, parameters and thresholds are described in detail (eight checks, seven injected defect classes, replay procedure, collapse definition, statistics), and archives and code are linked by DOI and on GitHub. Missing: the rule for choosing the 30 retail tasks (the text says only 'sampled'), and the model episodes cannot be regenerated because the provider offers no deterministic seed, so the package recomputes the analyses, not the runs.

    “A replication package recomputes every analysis offline with no model calls.”
    ✓ Checked: Searched the stored snapshot for 'sampled', 'random' and 'seed' on 2026-11-01. Every hit either repeats that 30 tasks were sampled or concerns the provider's missing seed; no selection rule is given in the paper itself.✓ Checked: Opened both Zenodo records and the GitHub repo in the browser on 2026-11-01. Record 23290600 (code, v1.0.0) lists SimGlot-v1.0.0.zip (2.6 MB); the zip preview lists artifact/ (oracle_audit, holdout, gates, experiments, harness incl. prompts.py, configs, scripts incl. reproduce.sh and analysis/scripts/offline_repro.sh), docs/ (reproduction.md, data_availability.md, limitations.md), manifests/, paper/, results/ and vendor/tau-bench. Record 23290703 (data) lists checksum.sha256, DATA_TERMS.md, README.md and four archives: simglot-S2-final, simglot-enhancement-final, simglot-v1.0-anon-final, simglot-v1.0-final (.tar.gz); I read its README but did not download or open the archives. (i) Task selection: artifact/configs/tasks/task_sample.json on GitHub states a rule (in Chinese): a frame of 91 scorable tasks filtered from the 115 test tasks by scripts/audit_tasks.py, 30 sampled, seed 20261007, stratified by intent family x complexity with largest-remainder quotas, a one-task floor for rare families, and a seeded draw within each cell; the 30 task_ids are listed. The rule is in the repo config, not in the paper text, and audit_tasks.py does not appear in the zip listing. (ii) Prompts: harness/prompts.py releases the user-simulator system prompt in English (verbatim tau-bench) and Chinese, and the English task instructions are in task_sample.json (tasks_zh.json is listed but I did not open it); the tool-calling agent prompts sit in the vendored tau-bench. (iii) Offline reproduction: artifact/scripts/reproduce.sh exists (no network, no model key); docs/reproduction.md says it runs the oracle audit, gates and Table 1 recomputation only, that exit 0 does not mean the paper is reproduced, and that the other experiments need the Zenodo archives (replay via scripts/offline_repro.sh inside the v1.0 archive). I did not run any script.
  • Stated limitationsStrong

    Section 6 names specific ways the findings could be wrong or not generalize (one benchmark, one provider, no seed, baseline from an earlier run period, 17% power on the smallest contrast) and acts on them: it reports the method failing on a second framework, a sign reversal in a second domain, two arms dropped after pilot data, and scoped claims.

    “The sign of the effect does not survive a change of task domain”
  • ActionabilityStrong

    Rating the main idea: add a repeat-scoring check (score a finished episode twice, require the same verdict) and a terminal-state check to the harness's own tests. The paper says neither needs a reference implementation, a model call or a human. The full suite and the replay correction need a harness that records tool calls and can reset its environment.

    “Neither requires a reference implementation, a model call, or a human.”
  • Interest disclosedStrong

    Academic author with no evident commercial stake. The paper states no funding and no competing interests. The author does release their own auditing suite, which is the paper's subject, not a product.

    “The author declares no competing interests.”
  • DurabilityStrong

    Rating the main idea: a grader is software that can mutate state and should be tested like software. That holds across models and benchmarks. The worked example is one benchmark's reward function and named model versions, which will age.

    “An evaluation oracle is software, and it needs the tests other software gets.”
03

Why Agentic Workflows Fail: A Taxonomy Derived from Literature and Empirical Data

Li, Bahsoon & Chen (University of Birmingham) · Oct 2026

Verdict. Included for the framing and the sample, not as a prevalence estimate. The trade-off is the authors' own caveat: the counts show what gets reported in papers and public issues, not how often agents fail, and the repair evidence is a 17-case exploratory look.

Scored by Claude (single pass, full text read). Second review: Independent blind pass, Opus, from the snapshot only (draft scores not shown); prompt blind-v1, no disagreements. Reviewed by the author.

  • Evidence basisStrong

    Reports 397 literature-derived records and 1,196 GitHub-derived episodes (from 467 issues and pull requests across 229 repositories), with the funnel from about 7,000 search hits and a 69-record hold-out from 12 later studies. Counts and denominators are given throughout. What was observed is coded failure reports, not measured failure rates.

    “Analysis of 397 literature-derived and 1,196 GitHub-derived failure records”
  • Method transparencyStrong

    Gives the databases, search string, inclusion and exclusion criteria, the fuzzy-match threshold (partial ratio of 80 or more), time-sliced queries, coding rules, agreement statistics and a replication package URL. The coding prompts and full codebook are held in that package, not in the paper, so reproducing depends on the package being reachable.

    “queries were time-sliced into three-month windows from mid-2023 onward and six-month windows before that”
    ✓ Checked: Opened the replication package in a browser on 2026-11-01: it loads (anonymous.4open.science, 45 files, shown as available until 28 Dec 2026). Prompts present: prompt/episode_prompt (episode and step splitting instructions) and prompt/judge_prompt (responsibility rules plus a failure dictionary). No file is named codebook; the failure dictionary is inside judge_prompt, and DATA_SCHEMA.md lists a 66-row 02_dictionary sheet in fine_data.xlsx. thesis_data/01_thesis.xlsx is the literature error dictionary. Other files seen: README.md, MANIFEST.md, DATA_SCHEMA.md, fine_data.xlsx, thesis_data/02_human.xlsx, 03_LLM.xlsx, 04_SLR_literature_collection_analysis.xlsx, six numbered pipeline scripts (01 to 06), keyword_vocabulary.txt, legacy_positional_keyword_matcher.py, a github_data folder (25 files). Not checked: the spreadsheet contents (so the 65-type count is unverified), and the host is time-limited.
  • Stated limitationsStrong

    A threats-to-validity section names specific ways the result could be wrong: step splitting is interpretive, one reviewer in the full double coding was an LLM, the GitHub sample is not random, and the source contrast cannot separate reporting bias from real incidence. It acts on these with cluster-bootstrap intervals, leave-one-repository-out checks, a temporal hold-out and scoped claims about visibility rather than prevalence.

    “cannot distinguish an unreported failure from an absent failure”
  • ActionabilityPartial

    The direction is clear: code each failure by responsible side, workflow stage and capability to route triage. It needs adaptation: the 65-type codebook and prompts live in the replication package, and the paper says it supplies no root-cause graph, tested fault-injection procedure or monitoring system. Missing: a ready-to-use triage checklist; the only repair evidence is an exploratory inspection of 50 episodes, 17 with a visible fix.

    “It does not itself supply a root-cause graph, a tested fault-injection procedure, or a deployed monitoring system.”
  • Interest disclosedStrong

    Academic authors proposing a research taxonomy; no product or commercial tool is recommended. No funding, conflict or competing-interest statement appears in the captured article text.

    ✓ Checked: Checked in a browser on 2026-11-01. arXiv abstract page: title, three authors, abstract, submission history, no funding or conflict text, and no affiliations. Full-text HTML (arxiv.org/html/2610.17418): front matter gives School of Computer Science, University of Birmingham, one author email; section headings are Introduction, Related Work, Methodology, Taxonomy, Empirical Study Results, Discussion, Threats to Validity, Conclusion, Data Availability, References, with no Acknowledgements, Funding or Competing Interests section. Text search for fund, grant, sponsor, conflict, competing, declar, ethic found only the arXiv site banner about its own funders. PDF view rendered blank in the browser and could not be read; the HTML is the same paper. Negative finding confirmed for the HTML version.
  • DurabilityStrong

    Coding failures by responsible side, stage and capability does not depend on a model, tool or vendor. The frequency tables are a snapshot (GitHub data to August 2025) and the auxiliary reviewer was a specific model, but the main idea is tied to neither.
